How estimates and approvals work

  1. You tell us what the car is doing, at the counter, by phone or with the form.
  2. We check the car and write up an estimate.
  3. We call or text you with the price. Nothing is repaired until you say yes.
  4. If we find something else, we call again before doing more work.
  5. Ask for your old parts back at drop-off and we'll keep them for you.

Our shop warranty and your factory warranty

Our parts-and-labor warranty terms are printed on every estimate. Ask for a copy before you approve the work.

Having routine maintenance done at an independent shop doesn't by itself void your manufacturer's warranty. Keep your receipts; we give you an itemized invoice every time.
