Check-in, check-out and late arrival

  • Check-in from 3pm. Check-out by 11am.
  • The front desk is open 7am to 11pm.
  • Arriving after 11pm? Call the desk before 11pm and we'll leave your key at the night window by the office.

Good to know before you book

  • Free parking at your door, with room for a boat or work trailer in the back lot.
  • Wi-Fi in every room. All rooms are non-smoking.
  • Room 101 is on the ground floor with a wider door and grab bars. Call to ask about its exact features.
  • Weekly rates: ask the desk.

Cancellation

Cancel by 4pm on your arrival day and you pay nothing. After that, the first night is charged. (A made-up policy for this example; a client's site quotes the owner's own policy.)

What's nearby

  • Sampletown Springs state park: 3 miles east.
