What's included in a rebuild

Built for phones

Designed for the phone your customers are holding, then opened up for desktop.

Your real content

We reuse and tidy your existing text, services, hours and photos you own. Nothing replaced with filler.

Secure and fast

A valid security certificate (the padlock in the browser) and a light site that loads quickly.

Working contact path

Click-to-call, email, a map link and a contact form that sends a pre-filled email to you — all tested.

A quote button on your site

A "Get a quote" button in the header of every page of your new site that opens your contact form.

Google Business Profile check

At launch we check that the name, phone, hours and website link on your Google Business Profile match your new site, and tell you about any mismatches. We don't log in to or edit your profile.

Review link

A "Leave us a review" button that opens your own Google review page: a review link you can share with all your customers.

Search basics

Page titles, descriptions, your business details in a form Google can read, and a link to your Google Business Profile.

Accessibility basics

Readable contrast, keyboard navigation and image descriptions. (We don't promise legal "ADA compliance".)

Not included: online stores, booking systems or logo design. Rewritten wording comes with Refresh Plus. We can quote other work separately in writing.

Questions

Why no calls?

We work entirely by email so everything is in writing and you can reply when it suits you. Most small-business sites can be scoped from a short questionnaire.

What if I only need a few pages?

Refresh is for sites of up to 5 pages, at $499 one-time. Your written scope lists the exact pages before you pay anything.

What if the preview isn't right?

You get the revision rounds in your scope: one with Refresh, two with Refresh Plus. And if you decide you don't want the site to go live, tell us by email any time before launch and we refund what you paid in full (Refresh and Refresh Plus). Full terms

Do I keep my domain and email?

Yes. Your domain stays registered in your name. When we launch, we change only the website records and leave your email settings alone.

Who owns the finished site?

Your content is yours. Once the rebuild is paid in full, you can take the site files with you if you move to another provider.

Can you work with my WordPress, Wix or Squarespace site?

We rebuild sites as fast, simple sites hosted for you on Site Care. If you skip Site Care, you get the files and simple hosting steps. If you need to stay on your current platform, tell us and we'll say honestly whether we can help.

Will my old page links still work?

We keep your existing page addresses working: any page that moves is pointed to its new address, and we test every old link after launch. No one can promise search rankings, and Google says some movement is normal for a few weeks after any redesign. We'll show you the before-and-after link check.

How are sites built?

We use templates and automated tools. Every page is checked with automated tests and against your written scope before you see it. We'll always tell you how something was made if you ask.

How do I pay?

Only after you approve your free homepage mockup and agree to your written scope and price. Refresh and Refresh Plus: in full, by card through a secure payment link we email you. Custom: 50% by Stripe invoice when you approve your mockup and 50% at launch. Site Care starts at launch. We never ask for card details by email.
