Getting your files

How do I get my files after I buy?

It depends on the product.

  • Paycheck Planner and Paycheck Planner Plus Bundle: right after you pay, Stripe's confirmation screen shows real links to your Google Sheets copy and the Excel (.xlsx) file. Plus Bundle purchases also include The January Money Reset PDF. Please save those files now.
  • Holiday and year-round printables: after a successful payment, Stripe's confirmation screen shows your download link. Please save the file now.
  • Website Check-Up: we email your report to the address on the order. We aim to send it within about 3 business days. That is a target, not a promise.
  • Refresh, Refresh Plus, Custom, and Site Care work is handled by email, not as an instant download.

If you need the links or files again, email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the email address you used at checkout and the product you bought. Never send card details.

I didn't get my file. What do I do?
  1. Wait a few minutes, then check Spam or Junk, and in Gmail the Promotions tab, for your receipt.
  2. Look again at the Stripe confirmation screen if you still have that tab open, and save the files from there.
  3. Still nothing? Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the email address you used at checkout and the product name. We'll send a fresh link or a working copy. You do not need to ask for a refund to get that help.
The download link doesn't work.

Try the link again in a different browser. If you were on a phone, try a computer. If a Google Drive link asks you to request access, or a Sheets link will not make a copy, email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the email you used at checkout and the product name. We'll send a fresh link.

I typed the wrong email address at checkout.

Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com from the address you want to use. Include the name on the order and the address you typed by mistake, and we'll get the files to the right inbox.

Can I download the files again later?

Yes. Keep the confirmation links, or keep the email if we re-send them. If you lose them, just ask.

Do I need an account or a login?

No CyberDad Kit account is required. For the Paycheck Planner or Plus Bundle, a free Google account is needed if you use the Google Sheets copy. The Excel file can be used without Google. Printable PDFs open with a normal PDF reader. There is no member login.

Refunds

What if a digital product isn't right for me?

For Paycheck Planner, the Plus Bundle, and the holiday and year-round printables named above, you may request a full refund for any reason within 7 days of your purchase date. Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the email address used for purchase and your order reference. Never send card details. Refunds are returned to the original payment method. No physical return is required. This does not limit your statutory rights. Requests made more than 7 days after purchase are reviewed case by case for Paycheck Planner, the Plus Bundle, and the holiday printables.

The planner and bundle policy is at https://getcyberdadkit.com/paycheck-planner/refund/. The holiday printable policy is at https://getcyberdadkit.com/refund-policy/. Year-round printable policies are at https://getcyberdadkit.com/planner/2027/refund/ (2027 Family Planner), https://getcyberdadkit.com/screen-free/refund/ (Screen-Free Kids Activity Bundle), and https://getcyberdadkit.com/journal/2027/refund/ (2027 Bible Verse Journal).

How do refunds and cancellation work for website projects, the Check-Up, and Site Care?

Those terms live at https://getcyberdadkit.com/sites/refund/. In short: Refresh and Refresh Plus are covered by the Love the Preview promise, including a full refund if you decide before launch that you do not want the site to go live. Custom work has different rules after work starts. The Check-Up is refunded in full if we cannot deliver the report. Site Care is month to month; we don't refund partial months. Full terms are at https://getcyberdadkit.com/sites/refund/.

How long does a refund take to show up?

Once a refund is issued, it is returned to your original payment method. On website projects, this is usually within 5 to 10 business days after we issue the refund. For digital products, banks often take several business days to show it. That timing varies by bank.

You can unsubscribe from those updates with one click. We don't sell your personal information. The privacy policy is at https://getcyberdadkit.com/cyberdadkit-privacy-policy.html.
