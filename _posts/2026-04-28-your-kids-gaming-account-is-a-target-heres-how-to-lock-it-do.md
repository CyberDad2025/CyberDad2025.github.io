## Your Kid's Gaming Account Is a Target — Here's How to Lock It Down

Gaming accounts on Roblox, Fortnite, Minecraft, PlayStation and Xbox hold payment details, friends lists and in-game purchases, which makes them worth stealing. The good news: a few settings make an account much harder to take over.

**Does this affect my family?**
A hacked gaming account can expose your payment info, your child's personal details, and even be used to scam their friends.

**What to do right now:**
1. Turn on two-factor authentication on every gaming account your child uses
2. Make sure the account email is one you control, not your kid's personal email
3. Remove saved credit cards from gaming platforms
4. Set spending limits or require parental approval for purchases
5. Talk to your kid about never sharing their password — even with friends
6. Use a unique password for each gaming account, ideally stored in a password manager
7. Be wary of messages offering free in-game currency or items; they are a common way to steal logins

A little setup now prevents a major headache.
