Save your Paycheck Planner files

Thank you for your purchase. Your file links are on Stripe's confirmation screen right after you pay. A few quick steps keep them safe.

  1. Download now. Save the Excel (.xlsx) file and, if you bought the Plus Bundle, the PDF guide to your computer.
  2. Add the Google Sheets copy to your Drive. Choose Make a copy so the sheet lives in your own Google Drive and you can edit it freely.
  3. Keep your Stripe receipt. It shows the email address and order reference we will ask for if you need help.

Lost your files? Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with your receipt and we'll resend them.
