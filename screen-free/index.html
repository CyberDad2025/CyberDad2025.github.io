Why it helps

The food is still a while away, the rain will not let up, or the drive is long. Instead of reaching for your phone, slide a printed page across the table. The Screen-Free Kids Activity Bundle gives you 34 pages to print at home for exactly those moments: puzzles to solve, games to play together and pages to draw and color, with answer keys for you. Print at home. Nothing is shipped.

At the restaurant. You sit down, order, and the wait begins. Pull out a folded page: six tic-tac-toe grids, a busy-table look-and-find with cups, forks and stars to count, a blank pizza to fill with made-up toppings, a category sprint and a dots-and-boxes grid. A pencil goes back and forth across the table until the plates arrive, and your phone can stay in your pocket.

On a rainy afternoon. Nobody can go outside, and everyone is getting restless. Hand over the Great Puddle Parade fill-in story, a blueprint page for planning a blanket fort, an indoor scavenger hunt with 18 things to find, four drawing prompts and a six-panel make-your-own comic. Then they can go build the fort they just drew.

In the car, on the bus or on the train. Rest the road spotting board on a book and let them hunt out the window for a red car, a bridge, a water tower or someone waving. When they finish a row, there is a road trip word search and a winding road maze waiting.

Anytime. For the quiet stretch before dinner or a slow morning, pick a maze, a secret code to crack, a rocket to connect dot by dot or a coloring page, and let them settle in. The full list is below.

Every activity page is marked EASY or TRICKIER, so you can pick one that fits, and the answer keys are at the back for you. When the stack is done, there is a Puzzle Champion certificate to fill in.

A look inside

Print at home

  • Print on plain US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) paper, portrait, at 100% or Actual size. If your printer clips the edges, choose Fit to printable area.
  • Choose the color PDF, or the low-ink PDF (black and gray, no background fills) to save toner. Coloring pages are line art in both.
  • No cutting needed. A pencil makes the mazes easy to erase.
  • Print only the pages you want, as often as you want, for your own personal use. Keep a few in a bag, the car or a kitchen drawer.
