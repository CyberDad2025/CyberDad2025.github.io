A look inside

Holidays

The year view and monthly calendars circle the 11 U.S. federal holidays on the dates observed in 2027, as published by the U.S. Office of Personnel Management (OPM), and the weekly pages name them. You can add your own family dates and local observances on the important-dates pages.

Printing tips

  • Print portrait at actual size on plain US Letter paper. Single-sided works best; double-sided is fine if your printer supports it.
  • Everything stays inside half-inch (12.7 mm) margins.
  • Print only the months and weeks you need next, plus extra copies of any page you use often.

Tablet app compatibility

Use the tablet edition in a PDF note-taking app that supports handwriting and internal PDF links. Some apps follow links only in a read or view mode, so switch modes if a tap starts writing instead of jumping. App features vary, so try a few links before you start writing. The bookmarks give a second way to move between pages.
