How to get help

For a missing download, a link that doesn't work, a file that won't open, a typo in the email you used at checkout, or a refund request, start with an email to ruel@getcyberdadkit.com. You can also reply to any email we have already sent you.

We don't ask for passwords, and we never need to connect to your devices. If anyone claiming to be CyberDadKit asks for either, it isn't us.

Response time

We read every email. We aim to reply within one business day. That is a target, not a promise.

What to include in your message

Please include:

  • the email address you used at checkout
  • the product you bought (or your order reference, if you have it)
  • a short description of what you need: a re-send, a link that doesn't work, a file that won't open, a refund, or something else

Never send card details, passwords, or one-time codes.

If your download link doesn't work

  1. Open the link again. After you pay, it appears on Stripe's confirmation screen.
  2. Try a different browser.
  3. If you were on a phone, try a computer.
  4. If the file still won't open, or Drive asks you to request access, email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the email you used at checkout and the product name. We'll send a fresh link.

If you closed the confirmation screen before saving the file, email us for a re-send. You do not need to ask for a refund to get a working copy.

Refunds

You may request a full refund for any reason within 7 days of your purchase date. See the refund policy for the full terms, and the FAQ for other common questions.
