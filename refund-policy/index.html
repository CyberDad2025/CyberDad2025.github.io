7-day full refund

You may request a full refund for any reason within 7 days of your purchase date.

Email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with:

  • the email address you used at checkout, and
  • your order reference, if you have it.

A short note about what didn't work is welcome, but not required. Never send card details.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method. No physical return is required, because there is nothing to mail back. Once a refund is issued, it may take some time for your bank to show it. Please delete your copy of the files and do not share them.

This policy does not promise automatic processing or a reply by a set time. It does not limit your statutory rights. For a bundle, the 7-day window covers the whole bundle purchase.

Requests made more than 7 days after purchase are reviewed case by case.

If a file or link doesn't work

If your download link doesn't work, a file won't open, or something looks incomplete, email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com with the email address you used at checkout. We'll send a fresh link or a working copy. You do not need to ask for a refund to get that help.

Who can buy

These products are available only to customers located in the United States. Checkout asks you to state that and to enter a billing address. Other billing countries can still be selected.

Printing license

You may print copies for your own personal, noncommercial gatherings, including internal office use. This license does not include resale of the files, rehosting the files, or a commercial event service.
