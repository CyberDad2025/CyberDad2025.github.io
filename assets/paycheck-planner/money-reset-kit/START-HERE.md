# Start Here: your Money Reset Kit

Thanks for getting the Money Reset Kit. Here is the fastest way in.

1. **Open the spreadsheet.** Use the Google Sheets copy link (it makes your own private copy), or open `CyberDadKit-Money-Reset-Kit-Planner.xlsx` in Excel 2016 or newer.
2. **Open the Days 1-30 tab.** It shows one short task for each day and links to the tab you need. Type Yes in Done when a day is finished. Type your numbers on Settings, Bills and One-Time, not on the Sample tab.
3. **Watch the walkthroughs** in `videos/`. They are silent with captions, so you can watch anywhere. Chapter 1 covers setup. The other four chapters follow the days in the plan.
4. **Print the checklists.** The 30-day tracker and the weekly 15-minute checkup are at the back of `CyberDadKit-30-Day-Money-Reset-Plan.pdf`, and also in `CyberDadKit-Money-Reset-Printable-Checklists.pdf`. US Letter paper.
5. **Do Day 1 today**, on the Settings tab.

## How the sample works

- Your payday, pay, bills and one-time costs start empty. Type your own next payday, bills and one-time costs there.
- Leave Numbers to use on Auto. The planner shows the sample until you type your payday, then it switches to yours. Pick Sample to look at the example again, or Mine to see only your numbers. Switching never deletes anything.
- Do not type on the Sample tab. It is a read-only example and is never erased.
- Pick USD or CAD, and a date style if you want one other than Month name.
- Debt Payoff, Savings Goals and Sinking Funds ship with sample rows in their yellow cells. Replace them with yours.

## Good to know

- Every figure in the sample is a sample number, made up for the example.
- Yellow cells are for typing. Grey cells are calculated for you.
- In real use, leave Today's date blank on Settings so the planner uses today.
- The Debt Payoff tab is a planning estimate. Real lenders may compute interest daily or charge fees.
- This kit is self-serve. It does not include coaching or live help, and it does not connect to your bank.
