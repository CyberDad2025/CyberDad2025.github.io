Cyber Dad Kit Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2, 2025 · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Cyber Dad Kit (“we,” “our,” or “us”) values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our digital products.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, shipping/billing information (if applicable)
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, clicks, and referring websites

How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver and support your purchases
  • To send updates, promotional material, and important alerts
  • To improve our product and services

We may use third-party tools like Google Analytics or email services that may collect data for analytics and marketing. These providers have their own privacy policies.

Purchases and Email Measurement

When you buy from CyberDadKit, our email and checkout provider MailerLite records your order (your email address, what you bought, the amount and the date), and Stripe processes your payment.

Our emails record when they are opened and which links are clicked. If you click a link in one of our emails and then buy within 30 days, MailerLite connects that order to the email you clicked so we can see which emails are actually helpful. We use this information only to deliver your purchase and improve CyberDadKit, and we never sell it. Separately, the MailerLite signup form on our homepage and some articles may store cookies or similar data in your browser (such as local storage) so the form works; see our Cookie Policy.

You can stop marketing emails at any time with the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email. See MailerLite's privacy policy and Stripe's privacy policy for how they handle data.

CyberDad Kit Sites Requests

This section covers the website request form and request assistant at /sites/ and the website intake questionnaire at /sites/intake/.

What the forms ask for. The request form and the request assistant ask for your business name, your current website address, what you want changed or don't like about your current site, the pages and features you need, style examples, a budget range, a timeline and your email address. The intake questionnaire asks about your business and the site you want: business names, website address, business category, the street address or service area, phone numbers, email address and hours to show, licenses or memberships, your goals and customers, pages, services, text notes, links to keep (including your Google review link, if you have one), customer reviews to quote, brand colors and style, a link to your photos, documents to post, the email address that should receive your new site's contact-form messages, domain and email provider details, your Site Care choice, and the names and email addresses of the people who can request changes or approve the site.

How your answers are sent. These pages don't send or store your answers. When you press the button to send, your own email app opens a pre-filled email addressed to ruel@getcyberdadkit.com, and nothing is sent until you press Send in that app. The pages don't use a form service or database, don't set cookies, and don't load Google Analytics, ads or the MailerLite signup form. "Download my answers" saves a text file on your own device only. Your email provider handles the email you send under its own privacy policy.

How we use it. We use what you send only to reply to you, prepare your quote or written scope, and build and support the site you ask for. Details you ask us to show on your new site, such as your business address, phone numbers, hours and reviews, will appear publicly on that site. A website request doesn't add you to the CyberDadKit newsletter or any mailing list, and we never sell your information.

What the forms don't ask for. The forms don't ask for passwords, logins or payment details, so please don't include them. The logo field on the questionnaire doesn't upload anything; we ask you to email logos and photos separately.

Keeping and deleting your request. We keep request emails and answers for 24 months after your last contact with us, then delete them, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. To see, correct or delete what we hold, email ruel@getcyberdadkit.com or write to CyberDadKit, PO Box 940061, Maitland, FL 32794, United States of America.
