Before you print

Print portrait, single-sided, at 100% / Actual size, on plain US Letter or A4 paper. Do not use borderless printing. Everything stays inside half-inch margins; if your printer clips the edges, choose Fit to printable area. No cutting or trimming is needed. The place card uses one fold line. Keep the answer-key pages (17-18) with the host.
