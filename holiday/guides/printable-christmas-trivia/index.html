Five sample questions from Round 1

Unchanged questions from Christmas Around the World. Play first, then peek.

  • Bringing a decorated evergreen tree indoors at Christmas began as a custom especially in which European country?

    A Egypt · B India · C Germany · D Brazil

  • Each year, which country gives London the large Christmas tree that stands in Trafalgar Square?

    A Spain · B Norway · C Greece · D Canada

  • In the Netherlands, Sinterklaas gifts are traditionally given on the evening of which date?

    A November 1 · B December 5 · C December 24 · D January 6

  • In Mexico, the nine nights of candlelit processions before Christmas are called what?

    A Carnaval · B Las Posadas · C La Tomatina · D Día de la Tierra

  • The giant straw Yule goat built each year in the city of Gävle is in which country?

    A Japan · B Chile · C Italy · D Sweden

Answers

Answers: 1 C Germany. 2 B Norway. 3 B December 5. 4 B Las Posadas. 5 D Sweden. The free PDF answer key includes a short fact under each answer.
