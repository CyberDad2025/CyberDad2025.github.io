Printable Christmas trivia for families

A quiet way to keep the table talking after dessert: print a round, split into teams, and read the questions aloud. No phone, app or internet is needed to play. Below are five real questions from Round 1 of CyberDadKit Christmas Trivia Night, plus how to run them tonight.

How to run one round

  1. Print the free Round 1 PDF (US Letter or A4) at 100% / Actual size. Keep the answer page with the host.
  2. Split into teams. One person reads each question; teams write a letter (A–D) on a shared sheet.
  3. Score 1 point per correct answer. Read the short fact under each answer from the key.
  4. If you want a longer night, the full pack has 8 rounds and 80 questions, team answer sheets, a scoreboard and two tie-breakers.

Five sample questions from Round 1

These are unchanged questions from Christmas Around the World (Round 1 of 8). Answers are at the bottom of this section so guests can play first.

  1. Bringing a decorated evergreen tree indoors at Christmas began as a custom especially in which European country?
    A Egypt · B India · C Germany · D Brazil
  2. Each year, which country gives London the large Christmas tree that stands in Trafalgar Square?
    A Spain · B Norway · C Greece · D Canada
  3. In the Netherlands, Sinterklaas gifts are traditionally given on the evening of which date?
    A November 1 · B December 5 · C December 24 · D January 6
  4. In Mexico, the nine nights of candlelit processions before Christmas are called what?
    A Carnaval · B Las Posadas · C La Tomatina · D Día de la Tierra
  5. The giant straw Yule goat built each year in the city of Gävle is in which country?
    A Japan · B Chile · C Italy · D Sweden

Answers: 1 C Germany. 2 B Norway. 3 B December 5. 4 B Las Posadas. 5 D Sweden. The free PDF answer key includes a short fact under each answer.
