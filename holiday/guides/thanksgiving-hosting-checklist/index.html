Thanksgiving hosting checklist, printable

Hosting works better when you count back from the meal day instead of staring at a calendar full of fixed dates. The checklist below is taken from page 3 of the CyberDadKit Thanksgiving Host Dinner Planner. It is undated, so you reuse it any year.

Countdown excerpt (from the free sample)

Check off what applies and add your own tasks on blank lines in the PDF.

  • 3 weeks out

    Pick the meal time and guest list. Choose host-cooked, potluck or a mix. Sketch the menu. Count chairs, plates and glasses. Order a turkey, if using one.

  • 2 weeks out

    Invite guests and start RSVPs. Share the potluck list. Gather recipes. Buy drinks and pantry items.

  • 1 week out

    Confirm RSVPs and dietary notes. Finish the shopping list. Plan seating. Clear fridge and freezer space. If the turkey is frozen, plan the thaw.

  • 2–3 days before

    Buy fresh produce, dairy and bread. Start make-ahead items. Wash serving dishes and linens. Label serving dishes.

  • The day before

    Set the table. Prep vegetables, sauces and pies. Chill drinks and clear the counters. Fill in the hour-by-hour cooking pages.

  • The day itself

    Follow the hour-by-hour plan. Check the turkey with a thermometer. Set out drinks and starters. Pack and label leftovers.

The sample PDF also restates a refrigerator-thawing fact from USDA FSIS on the checklist page. The planner is a planning tool; for current food-safety guidance, see fsis.usda.gov and foodsafety.gov.
