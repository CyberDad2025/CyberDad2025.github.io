Countdown excerpt

  • 3 weeks out

    Pick the meal time and guest list. Sketch the menu. Count chairs, plates and glasses.

  • 2 weeks out

    Invite guests and start RSVPs. Share the potluck list. Gather recipes.

  • 1 week out

    Confirm RSVPs and dietary notes. Finish the shopping list. Plan seating.

  • 2–3 days before

    Buy fresh produce, dairy and bread. Start make-ahead items. Label serving dishes.

  • The day before

    Set the table. Prep vegetables, sauces and pies. Fill in the hour-by-hour pages.

  • The day itself

    Follow the hour-by-hour plan. Check the turkey with a thermometer. Pack leftovers.
