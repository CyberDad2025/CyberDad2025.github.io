Before you print

Print portrait, single-sided, at 100% / Actual size, on plain US Letter or A4 paper. Do not use borderless printing. Everything stays inside half-inch margins; if your printer clips the edges, choose Fit to printable area. The low-ink editions are gray and black on white, with no background fills. Print extra copies of any page you need more of. A physical print test has not been performed. Print a test page first.
