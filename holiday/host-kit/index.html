Printing tips

  • Print each PDF on its matching paper (US Letter 8.5 x 11 in or A4 210 x 297 mm): portrait, single-sided, at 100% / Actual size, with no borderless printing. Use Fit to printable area if your printer clips the edges.
  • A physical print test has not been performed. Print a test page first.
  • The low-ink editions are black and gray on white, with no background fills.
  • No cutting or trimming is needed for the games, trivia or activity pages. Some pages use fold lines. The planner's leftover labels can be cut apart or used as a full sheet.
  • You need pens, pencils or crayons. Some Gather & Play games also use scrap paper, as listed on each game page.
  • Keep the answer-key pages with the host.
  • The tablet edition of the planner works in a PDF reader that supports handwriting and internal links. Link behavior depends on the reader.
