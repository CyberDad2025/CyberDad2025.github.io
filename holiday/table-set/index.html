Printing tips

  • Print landscape, single-sided, at 100% / Actual size, on plain US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) or A4 (210 x 297 mm) paper. Do not use borderless printing.
  • Everything stays inside half-inch (12.7 mm) margins. If your printer clips the edges, choose Fit to printable area.
  • The color editions have a cream background. The low-ink editions are gray and black outlines on white, with no background fills.
  • Cut on the solid lines (crop marks show the corners) and fold on the dashed lines. Heavier paper helps tent cards stand if your printer accepts it.
  • Print as many copies of any page as your gathering needs.
