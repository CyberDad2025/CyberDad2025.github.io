🍪 CyberDad Cookie Policy

How cookies are used on this site today

Last Updated: October 21, 2026

🤔 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. Some of the services this site uses set cookies so they can work and measure how the site is used.

🍪 How CyberDad Uses Cookies

🏠 Our Own Site

CyberDadKit is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. The site itself does not set its own cookies, and it does not show a cookie consent banner. The cookies described below come from the third-party services the site loads.

📊 Analytics Cookies

Provider: Google Analytics

Purpose: Help us understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it.

What they do:

  • Count visits and page views
  • Show which articles and pages are read
  • Record clicks, including clicks on the Shield Kit buy button
  • Show which websites send visitors here

Where: Loaded on most pages of the site

Duration: Set by Google; see Google's privacy policy below

📢 Advertising Cookies

Provider: Google AdSense

Purpose: Google's advertising script is loaded on most pages. Google may set cookies to show and measure ads.

Duration: Set by Google; see Google's privacy policy below

📧 Signup Form Cookies

Provider: MailerLite

Purpose: The email signup form on the homepage and on some articles is provided by MailerLite. MailerLite may set cookies so the form works. If you sign up, MailerLite also sends our emails.

Checkout: When you buy the Shield Kit, you leave this site for MailerLite's checkout page, which has its own policies.

🛠️ Managing Your Cookie Preferences

How to Control Cookies:

  • Browser Settings: You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Contact Us: Email us with any cookie or privacy question
